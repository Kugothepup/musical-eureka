    Why this answer?

    NOAA aurora model

    –

    NOAA’s OVATION model gives the chance of visible aurora for each patch of sky, about 30–90 minutes ahead. Aurora up to roughly 650 km away can show low on your horizon.

    Geomagnetic activity (Kp)–

    Kp runs from 0 to 9 and measures how disturbed Earth’s magnetic field is, in 3-hour blocks. Hatched bars are forecasts.

    AuroraWatch UK (Lancaster magnetometer)

    –

    Measures how much the magnetic field over the UK is being disturbed, in nanoteslas (nT).

    Solar wind (measured ~1.5 million km away)

    –

    Faster wind and a southward magnetic field (Bz below zero) feed the aurora. What’s measured here reaches Earth in about 30–60 minutes, so it’s an early warning.
